Fonts bundled with this project
===============================

Instrument Sans — instrument-sans-variable-latin.woff2
  Designers: Rodrigo Fuenzalida, Jordan Egstad
  Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1
  https://openfontlicense.org

DM Mono — dm-mono-500-latin.woff2
  Designer: Colophon Foundry
  Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1
  https://openfontlicense.org

Both families are redistributable under the OFL. Only the latin subset is
bundled (the full character set is not needed for Italian or English copy),
which keeps the two files at roughly 45 KB combined.

They are self-hosted rather than loaded from a CDN so that the pages work
offline and make no third-party requests.
